## 7. Review Request Link & Office Counter QR Code Standee

### Step A: Copy Your Official Google Review Link
1. Search **Visatrip Global** on Google while signed into the verified GBP Google account.
2. Click **Ask for reviews** (or inside GBP Manager: **Home → Get more reviews**).
3. Copy your short review link (format: `https://g.page/r/XXXXXXXXXXXX/review`).
4. Paste this link into `/src/config/site.ts` → `localSeo.gbpReviewUrl` and redeploy — this automatically activates the **"Leave a Google Review"** button on `/contact`.

### Step B: Generate & Print Your Office Counter QR Standee
1. Open `https://www.visatripglobal.com/admin/leads` and sign in with your `ADMIN_PASSWORD`.
2. Click the **Office Google Review QR Standee** tab.
3. Paste your `https://g.page/r/.../review` link into the input box.
4. Click **Print Standee** (fits cleanly inside an **A5 acrylic counter holder** at your Rohanpur desk) or download **PNG (1024px)** / **Vector SVG** to send to a local Chapai Nawabganj print shop.

### Step D: Google Review Compliance Rules
- Never offer cash, discounts, or gifts in exchange for reviews.
- Never post reviews on a client's behalf or ask multiple clients to submit reviews on the office Wi-Fi at the same time (ask them to scan with their own mobile data).
